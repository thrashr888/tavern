# Needs-Paul escalation

## When to use

Blocked on a Paul decision or credential

## Steps

1. State the decision in one line with your pick.
2. Put it in a Needs from Paul line.
3. Never ask for secrets in chat or email; use a vault link.
4. Keep working on unblocked parts.
