# Multi-agent review

## When to use

Work by one agent needs a second agent's check before it counts (plan, doc, PR, research)

## Steps

1. Author names the artifact, the claim to check and the acceptance check.
2. Reviewer reads the source, not the summary, and notes what was verified vs inferred.
3. Reviewer replies on the same thread with findings and a clear pass / changes needed.
4. Author fixes and re-requests.
5. Anything that loosens a rule or needs access goes to Paul as a decision.
6. Reviewer does not merge or send outward.

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.
