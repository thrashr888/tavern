Every 30 min (standing schedule)

## Steps

1. Triage inbox with a broadened query (smithery OR smithy OR tavern OR rookery OR agentkernel, plus mail from the party address).
2. Compare against the seen-ID watermark; read only new bodies, in full.
3. Surface only what needs Paul: new tasks, questions, blockers, decisions.
4. Update the watermark. Silence otherwise.
