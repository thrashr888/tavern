# Add email events to the calendar

## When to use

Email mentions an event or date (e.g. the "data outpost" event), on inbox scan

## Steps

1. Find the event in email: title, date, time, place, sender.
2. Verify weekday/date pairs; ask if they disagree.
3. Add to Paul's personal calendar without asking (standing grant, Sep 10). Family-shaped items also go on the family calendar; Em items per the placement rule.
4. If Paul has not confirmed he is going, mark it unconfirmed: title prefixed "Unconfirmed:" or tentative status, and say so in the description.
5. Service visits and club events: free (non-busy), location blank until confirmed; never copy a venue from an old event.
6. RSVPs and invites: ask Paul first.
7. Tell Paul what was added and flag collisions. Text Jenn about family calendar changes.
8. When Paul confirms, drop the Unconfirmed marker.

## Done when

Not written yet. Add it at the next Friday review.

## Tools

Gmail, Google Calendar

## Permissions

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.
