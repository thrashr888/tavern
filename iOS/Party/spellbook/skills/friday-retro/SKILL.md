# Friday retro

## When to use

Weekly, Fridays 9am PT

## Steps

1. Review how the week went: what shipped, stalled or broke (Linear boards, crew email threads, each member's own report).
2. What could have gone better: process friction, slow replies, misrouted work, quality.
3. Action items, each with an owner.
4. Follow up on last week's action items: done or not, and why.
5. New crew features and capabilities are one input, not the whole retro.
6. Consolidated report to Paul: wins, misses, improvements, action items with owners, status of prior items. Guild-casual tone, honest about misses.

Not written yet. Add it at the next Friday review.

Linear, Gmail (Smithery label), Retro tab in the Ways of Working doc

## Permissions

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.
