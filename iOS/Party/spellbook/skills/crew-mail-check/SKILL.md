# Crew mail check

## When to use

Hourly, or when Paul says there's new mail

## Steps

1. Search (from:Rook OR label:Smithery OR subject:Smithery).
2. Open every matching thread in full; search previews show only the oldest ~5 messages.
3. Act only on mail addressed to you or in your lane.
4. Reply in the existing thread, signed with your name (several agents share one address).
5. End with a Needs from Paul line.

Not written yet. Add it at the next Friday review.

## Tools

Gmail

## Permissions

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.
