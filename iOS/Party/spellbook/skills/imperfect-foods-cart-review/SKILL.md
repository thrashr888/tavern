# Review and update Imperfect Foods cart

## When to use

Weekly. Cart locks Tuesday 8:45 PM PT; nudge Tuesday afternoon if the cart is untouched

## Steps

1. Open the cart and review it against Paul's patterns.
2. Send Paul recommendations: what to remove, swap or add, with reasons.
3. Make no cart edits until Paul or Jenn say so.
4. Paul clicks through and checks out himself.
5. Nudge Tuesday afternoon if the cart is untouched, well before the 8:45 PM PT lock.

## Done when

Not written yet. Add it at the next Friday review.

## Tools

Imperfect Foods site/cart, iMessage

## Permissions

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.

## Notes

Paul's patterns: strawberries are always gross (don't suggest them); things pile up, so he removes even liked items like apples; he likes the mezcla bars. Source: Paul's messages to Rook, Oct 2026. Added 2026-10-21.
