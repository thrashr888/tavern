# Review and update the Spellbook

## When to use

Weekly, Fridays after the retro (confirmed by Paul 2026-10-21)

## Steps

1. Read `git log` on private-agent-skills since last Friday: new, changed and removed spells.
2. Fix stale spells and rituals, or retire one by deleting its folder in a pull request that says why.
3. Fold in lessons from the week's retro.
4. Mail each agent that can't read the repo its fresh bundle, with a short list of what changed.
5. Anything that would loosen a rule goes to Paul as a decision, not adopted by the crew.
6. Tell Paul only what changed or needs him.

Not written yet. Add it at the next Friday review.

## Tools

private-agent-skills repo, Linear, Gmail

## Permissions

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.
