# Inbox triage and draft-on-request

## When to use

Scheduled inbox check, or new mail that may need a reply

## Steps

1. Scan new mail in Paul's inbox (skip noise and Smithery crew threads).
2. List what needs a response: sender, the ask, any deadline.
3. Text Paul only if something needs him, and ask which ones to draft.
4. Draft only the ones he picks, in his voice, and show them.
5. Never send without his approval; outside the Smithery label, drafts only.
6. Track what was answered so nothing is nagged twice.

## Done when

Not written yet. Add it at the next Friday review.

## Tools

Gmail, iMessage

## Permissions

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.
