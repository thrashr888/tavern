# Clean up and archive old Smithery emails

## When to use

Daily Smithery sweep (~4pm), and when Paul asks for a cleanup

## Steps

1. Find Smithery-label threads that are done (work delivered, no open ask).
2. Archive them; never delete or trash.
3. Keep in the inbox anything with an open ask, appointment, medical, legal or tax content, or a Needs from Paul item.
4. If unsure whether a thread is done, leave it.
5. Check the thread is out of the inbox but still findable (label kept).
6. Tell Paul only the count and anything held back that needs him.

## Done when

Not written yet. Add it at the next Friday review.

## Tools

Gmail (Smithery label and sublabels)

## Permissions

This spell grants no authority. Follow Outward-action check (SB-12) and Paul's standing rules: ask Paul before anything outside the Smithery or anything irreversible, and keep secrets out of mail, docs and repos.

## Notes

Scope is Smithery threads only (Paul, Oct 4: archive, don't delete). Based on the standing daily sweep and his Gmail filter setup (Oct 15). Paul has not said adopted. Not for non-Smithery mail.
